About the Event

Faculty Career Day 2026 is an event organized to help students explore career opportunities, develop professional skills, and connect with industry professionals.

Guest Speaker: Software Engineer

An experienced software engineer will share insights about the technology industry, career pathways, and the skills required to become a successful developer.

Career Development Workshop

Students will learn how to prepare a professional resume, improve interview skills, and prepare for their future careers.
